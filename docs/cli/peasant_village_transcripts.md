## peasant village transcripts

Pull, list, and view transcripts from the Peasant village

### Synopsis

Pull a transcript (and its annotations) from the Peasant village, list pullable or already-pulled transcripts, and render a pulled transcript in the terminal.

Auth: `pull` and remote `list` contact the village and require login (run 'peasant village login'). `list --local` and `context` read only local data and work offline.
